# The Crooked Lantern

A muddy street. Lantern light spills from a crooked tavern door.

## Tone
Snarky goblins, guarded secrets, dark knights. Short scene — one amulet, one password, one way out.

## Hard flags
- Dewr's door tax is a scam (10 gold).
- Groll knows the storage password switch.
- Dremis holds the Amulet of Light in the storage room.
- Leaving without the amulet triggers a goblin ambush.


## Soft flags
- main_hall: There's a sleeping kitty by the fireplace
- main_hall: Sally will hum while working
- kitchen: Kitchen cook is a female goblin
